CSS Combinators

Combinator matlab do selectors ke beech ka rishta.

1. Descendant - div p

Div ke andar kahin bhi ho, sab p red honge.

Ye direct p hai - RED

Ye andar wala p hai - YE BHI RED

Ye bahar hai - RED NAHI

2. Child - div > p

Sirf direct bacha blue hoga.

Ye direct hai - BLUE

Ye andar wala hai - BLUE NAHI

3. Adjacent - h2 + p

Turant baad wala ek hi green hoga.

Heading

Turant baad - GREEN

Dusra p - GREEN NAHI

4. General - h2 ~ p

Baad wale saare orange honge.

Heading

Pehla - ORANGE

Beech me span

Dusra - YE BHI ORANGE
